# Everyday Wag

Frozen Raw with Chicken & Beef. Complete raw meal.

**Core attribute:** Four animal proteins for an easy everyday raw routine.

**Ingredients (working reference):** chicken, beef, beef bone, beef tripe,
chicken bone, whole salmon, pork, beef organs (lung, liver, heart, kidney,
spleen, pancreas), chicken organs (lung, liver, innards, heart, gizzard,
kidney, spleen, pancreas), beef bone broth, lettuce, red bell pepper, green
bell pepper, cucumber, zucchini, chicken feathers, activated charcoal
(added as a denaturing agent -- not a nutritional or health benefit).

**Published nutrition (working reference, per source product):**
- Calories: 1565 kcal/kg; 44 kcal/oz
- Crude protein: 15.3% min
- Crude fat: 8.98% min
- Carbohydrates: <0.50 g/100 g
- Crude fiber: 0.30% max
- Moisture: 70.8%
- Probiotics: 411,075 CFU/oz

**Format:** Frozen raw complete dog food. Ready to serve after safe
thawing; keep frozen until use, thaw in the refrigerator, keep
refrigerated after thawing, and wash hands/bowls/utensils/surfaces after
handling.

**Sizes:** 2 lb, 10 lb, and 38 lb (the 10 lb and 38 lb sizes are not
pre-portioned).

**Summary:** A ready-to-serve frozen raw formula featuring chicken, beef,
salmon and pork, together with bone, organs, broth and vegetables.
