# Beef To-Go

Freeze-Dried Raw Beef Meal. Complete raw meal (ground powder format).

**Core attribute:** Beef, bone, tripe and organs -- ready wherever the
bowl goes.

**Ingredients (working reference):** beef, beef bone, beef tripe, beef
organs (lung, liver, heart, kidney, spleen, pancreas), activated
charcoal (added as a denaturing agent -- not a nutritional or health
benefit).

**Published nutrition (working reference, per source product):**
- Calories: 139.75 kcal/oz
- Protein: 48.65% min
- Fat: 37.67% min
- Carbohydrates: <1.48 g/100 g
- Fiber: 13.05% max
- Moisture: 33.71%

**Format:** Freeze-dried complete raw dog food, ground powder. Store in a
cool, dry place and keep the package properly sealed. Scoop, portion,
sprinkle, or hydrate with water before serving. As a rough guide, 4 oz of
this powder is approximately equal to 1 lb of raw beef blend.

**Sizes:** 14 oz bag (approximately 3.5 lb raw-equivalent).

**Summary:** A ground freeze-dried raw powder made with beef, beef bone,
tripe and organs for convenient measuring, serving and storage.
