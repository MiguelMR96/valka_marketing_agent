# Quail Confetti

Freeze-Dried Whole Quail Egg Topper. **Bowl topper -- a supplement, not a
complete meal.** Do not recommend this as a dog's primary/blend-percentage
food; it's meant to be added to a meal, not replace one. Fed
intermittently or as a supplement.

**Core attribute:** Whole quail egg, ready to sprinkle over the bowl.

**Ingredients (working reference):** 100% whole quail eggs (yolk, white,
membrane, shell). No activated charcoal is added to this non-meat
topper.

**Published nutrition (working reference, per source product):**
- Calories: 126.43 kcal/oz
- Crude protein: 37.29% min
- Crude fat: 30.98% min
- Carbohydrates: 4.5%
- Crude fiber: 1.92% max
- Moisture: 3.19%

**Format:** Freeze-dried whole quail egg powder, supplemental topper.
Store in a cool, dry place and keep the package properly sealed.

**Sizes:** 2 oz bag.

**Feeding guidance:** cats and small dogs under 20 lb: 1 tsp/day; medium
dogs 20-50 lb: 1-2 tsp/day; large dogs 50+ lb: 2-3 tsp/day. Sprinkle over
kibble, wet, or raw food.

**Summary:** A single-ingredient topper made from whole quail egg,
including yolk, white, membrane and shell, simply freeze-dried for easy
serving.
