# Turkey Twist

Frozen Raw with Beef & Turkey. Complete raw meal.

**Core attribute:** Beef first, with turkey, salmon and pork for more bowl
variety.

**Ingredients (working reference):** beef, turkey, beef bone, beef organs
(lung, liver, heart, kidney, spleen, pancreas), turkey bone, turkey
organs (lung, liver, innards, heart, gizzard, kidney, spleen, pancreas),
whole salmon, beef tripe, pork, lettuce, red bell pepper, green bell
pepper, cucumber, zucchini, turkey feathers, activated charcoal (added
as a denaturing agent -- not a nutritional or health benefit).

**Published nutrition (working reference, per source product):**
- Calories: 1249 kcal/kg; 35.41 kcal/oz
- Crude protein: 14.56% min
- Crude fat: 8.69% min
- Carbohydrates: <0.50 g/100 g
- Crude fiber: 1.1% max
- Moisture: 73.72%

**Format:** Frozen raw complete dog food. Ready to serve after safe
thawing; keep frozen until use, thaw in the refrigerator, keep
refrigerated after thawing, and wash hands/bowls/utensils/surfaces after
handling.

**Sizes:** 2 lb, 10 lb, and 38 lb (the 10 lb and 38 lb sizes are not
pre-portioned).

**Summary:** A beef-first frozen raw formula with turkey, salmon and
pork, complemented by bone, organs, tripe and vegetables.
