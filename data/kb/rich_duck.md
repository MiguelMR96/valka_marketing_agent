# Rich Duck

Frozen Raw with Duck & Beef. Complete raw meal.

**Core attribute:** Our richest frozen blend by guaranteed minimum fat.

**Ingredients (working reference):** duck, duck organs (lung, liver,
innards, heart, gizzard, kidney, spleen, pancreas), duck bone, beef, beef
bone, beef tripe, beef organs (lung, liver, heart, kidney, spleen,
pancreas), whole salmon, lettuce, red bell pepper, green bell pepper,
cucumber, zucchini, beef bone broth, raw goat milk, duck feathers,
activated charcoal (added as a denaturing agent -- not a nutritional or
health benefit).

**Published nutrition (working reference, per source product):**
- Calories: 1252 kcal/kg; 35.48 kcal/oz
- Crude protein: 16.5% min
- Crude fat: 12.7% min
- Carbohydrates: <6.7 g/100 g
- Crude fiber: 0.25% max
- Moisture: 64.3%

**Format:** Frozen raw complete dog food. Ready to serve after safe
thawing; keep frozen until use, thaw in the refrigerator, keep
refrigerated after thawing, and wash hands/bowls/utensils/surfaces after
handling.

**Sizes:** 2 lb and 10 lb (the 10 lb size is not pre-portioned). A 38 lb
size is not currently available for this formula -- availability,
production cost and price have not been confirmed.

**Summary:** A duck-first frozen raw formula with beef and salmon, plus
organs, bone, tripe, bone broth, raw goat milk and vegetables.
