# Goat Splash

Frozen Raw Goat Milk Topper. **Bowl topper -- a supplement, not a
complete meal.** Do not recommend this as a dog's primary/blend-percentage
food; it's meant to be added to a meal, not replace one.

**Core attribute:** Pourable raw goat milk for an easy bowl upgrade.

**Ingredients (working reference):** raw goat milk. No activated charcoal
is added to this non-meat topper.

**Published nutrition (working reference, per source product):**
- Crude protein: 2% min
- Crude fat: 1.4% min
- Crude fiber: 0.26% max
- Crude moisture: 92% max
- Calories: not published on the source page

No complete-and-balanced adequacy statement is published for this
product -- it's described as a topper, treat, or supplemental addition,
suitable for adult dogs and cats as well as puppies and kittens.

**Format:** Frozen liquid raw goat milk, supplemental topper for dogs
and cats. Keep frozen, thaw in the refrigerator, keep refrigerated after
thawing. Must carry the applicable pet-food-only warning; not for human
consumption.

**Sizes:** 16 oz and 32 oz bottles.

**Summary:** A one-ingredient raw goat milk topper for dogs and cats,
bottled fresh and delivered frozen for safe cold-chain handling.
