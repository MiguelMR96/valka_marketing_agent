# Goat Cloud

Freeze-Dried Raw Goat Milk Powder. **Bowl topper -- a supplement, not a
complete meal.** Do not recommend this as a dog's primary/blend-percentage
food; it's meant to be added to a meal, not replace one.

**Core attribute:** Scoop it. Sprinkle it. Rehydrate it.

**Ingredients (working reference):** 100% raw goat milk. No activated
charcoal is added to this non-meat topper.

**Published nutrition (working reference, per source product):**
- Calories: 161.49 kcal/oz
- Crude protein: 30.76% min
- Crude fat: 38.45% min
- Crude fiber: 1.54% max
- Crude moisture: 3% max

No complete-and-balanced adequacy statement is published for this
product -- treat it as an intermittent or supplemental topper, not a
stand-alone diet.

**Format:** Freeze-dried raw goat milk powder, supplemental topper. Store
in a cool, dry place and keep the package properly sealed.

**Sizes:** 8 oz bag (approximately 32 tablespoons; 1 oz of powder equals
about 8 oz of raw goat milk).

**Feeding guidance:** under 20 lb: 1 tbsp/day; 20-40 lb: 1-2 tbsp/day;
40-80 lb: 2-4 tbsp/day; 80+ lb: 4+ tbsp/day. Mix into food, or add warm
water to reconstitute.

**Summary:** A single-ingredient freeze-dried raw goat milk powder that
can be measured, sprinkled over food, or reconstituted with water.
